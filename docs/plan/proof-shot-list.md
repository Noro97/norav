# Proof App shot list

For the owner to record, about 30–45 minutes of effort. S6 edits and compresses the result.

## Setup

- Use a test account on your own phone, in Telegram, against the current test environment.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb and hide notifications.
- Don't show other people's names, faces, videos or usernames. If a leaderboard shows real people, skip that shot.
- Don't complete a real payment. Stop at the Telegram Stars confirmation screen.
- Record in portrait with iOS Screen Recording, with no microphone.
- Film yourself, or a friend who agreed, for the exercise clip. Keep faces out of frame if you prefer.

## Shots

Keep each one short, and retake any that hesitate. They don't need to be one continuous take.

| # | Show | Length | Proves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open `@proof_app_bot` and launch the Mini App | 5s | It's a real Telegram app |
| 2 | Home screen with points, streak and level | 5s | The product loop |
| 3 | Choose an activity and record a short set of reps | 10–15s | The core action |
| 4 | Upload progress while the video compresses on the device | 5s | FFmpeg.wasm on the device |
| 5 | The verdict: reps counted, form and liveness | 10s | The Gemini verification |
| 6 | Points awarded, streak or level moving | 5s | The XP economy |
| 7 | Telegram Stars purchase screen, stopped before confirming | 5s | Stars payments |
| 8 | Bonus: a rejected attempt, if you can trigger one honestly | 5–10s | It says no as well |

## Also useful, if quick

- Screenshot of each of shots 2, 5 and 7.
- Your phone's screen size, so S6 can frame the clips correctly.

## Hand-off

Put the raw files in `~/Desktop/noro/portfolio/proof-footage/` and give S6 that path. Don't commit raw footage.

If a shot can't be recorded honestly, because the test environment no longer works, for example, say so. The case study will use what's real and say the app is a closed beta that has ended.
